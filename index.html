Grab an Aircoach or taxi to Dublin Airport for your evening flight back to Boston. Leave plenty of time - recommend 3 hours before international flights. Slán abhaile! (Safe home!)

💡 Top Tips for Your Trip:
  • Book Ahead: Reserve tables at Chapter One, The Winding Stair, and popular spots
  • Cash: Some traditional pubs prefer cash, so have euros on hand
  • Weather: Pack layers and a rain jacket - Dublin weather is unpredictable!
  • Walking Shoes: Dublin is very walkable - comfortable shoes are essential
  • Tipping: 10-12% is standard in restaurants (check if service charge included)
  • Pubs: Order at the bar in traditional pubs, don't wait for table service
  • Leap Card: Get a public transport card if using buses/trams
  • Temple Bar: Fun but touristy - we've included better spots too!

Have an amazing time in Dublin! 🍀

Sláinte! (Cheers!)
